# Kingdomino: The Lost Treasures

Mini expansion (2-4 players, 8+, ~20 min) by Bruno Cathala & Pauline Detraz.
Requires the Kingdomino base game to play.

> Rumors are spreading throughout the kingdom: mysterious treasure chests filled
> with gemstones have appeared throughout the kingdom! There's no time to lose,
> set out in search of these fabulous treasures!

## Components

- **16 Treasure Tokens.** Each token has a **Chest** side and a **Gem** side.
  There are 5 types of gems, with 3 identical tokens of each type. The 16th token
  is a **Joker**.

## Additional setup

(After setting up the Kingdomino base game.)

Place the 16 Treasure tokens **Chest-side up** next to the game box. Then randomly
flip **2** of them to their **Gem** side.

## Expansion rule

If, on their turn, a player creates a **square of four adjacent different
landscapes** when placing their domino, that player discovers a treasure!

To do so, they:

1. Choose one of the two visible Gem tokens.
2. Place it at the **intersection of their four landscapes**, orienting it in the
   direction of their choice.
3. Reveal a new Treasure token to its Gem side, so that there are always **two**
   Gem tokens visible.

Each Gem token contains **crowns**, with a specific distribution depending on the
gem color. Choose the orientation of the Gem token carefully when placing it, as
these crowns are **added to those already present on the dominoes** during
end-game scoring.

Once placed, a Gem token **cannot be moved or rotated**.

### Gem token faces

A Gem token points in one direction. Crown distribution varies by gem color; the
values printed across the gem types include 1, 2, and 4 crowns (e.g. a face worth
1 crown counted on 2 territories, up to 4 crowns counted on 2 territories), and
some faces are worth 0 crowns.

### Important: the skull

A territory **pointed at by a skull** will **not score any points** at the end of
the game. It has a value of 0, regardless of how many crowns it contains.

## End of the game

The game ends as usual once players have placed their last domino. Score points as
in the base game, taking into consideration the **additional crowns granted by Gem
tokens**. The player with the highest total score wins.

## Immediate victory

If a player manages to obtain **5 different gem types** in their kingdom, they
**immediately win the game**, without calculating points.

The **Joker** token counts as a gem of **any type**, making immediate victory
easier to achieve. However, it only provides **one crown**.
